Public health in five models

Built by Jay Kapoor as a companion to StrayPaw.

StrayPaw works on making animal birth control and rabies vaccination records easier to verify. Building that software raises questions a register alone cannot answer. Where did a campaign miss dogs? When did a change in reporting look like a change in incidence? How would we tell whether an intervention made a difference?

These experiments make those questions tangible. Each model starts from an aggregate in StrayPaw’s Supabase register. The notes show how those inputs are calculated, where hypothetical assumptions enter, and what extra evidence would be needed to make a real-world estimate.
