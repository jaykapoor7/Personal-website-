# Creative direction review

These are interaction studies on `concept-prototypes-2026-10-17`. They do not replace the current homepage or other production pages.

## 01 / The Desk

A personal workspace built from actual photographs and documents. In the playful state, visitors can pick up and move items. The switch settles those same items into an editorial index, while the tabletop becomes paper. This makes the two modes spatially related and can eventually give finished experiments a physical place without presenting utilities as games.

**Strength:** most personal and grounded in real assets; the mode switch has a clear object-to-index metaphor. **Risk:** too many desk objects could become clutter. Keep a strict content limit and let finished experiments open their own worlds.

## 02 / The Atlas

Places representing real site destinations form an explorable field. Clicking a place changes the reading panel. The mode switch draws the places into a clean ordered index and quiets the field. It is strongest as a navigational environment.

**Strength:** cohesive whole-window atmosphere and a legible two-way transformation. **Risk:** connections need a more specific conceptual meaning before becoming the final identity. Otherwise the field can read as abstract decoration.

## 03 / The Press

A small typesetting surface lets visitors stamp J, A, or Y onto the page. The switch aligns the loose letters and exposes a quiet professional page beneath them. The interaction works with pointer, touch, and keyboard.

**Strength:** the simplest memorable action and strongest typographic identity. **Risk:** the playful side revolves around the name rather than future experiments, so it needs a thoughtful way to introduce distinct interactive destinations.

## 04 / Project transformations

Twelve SVG pieces form a paw for StrayPaw, a care cross for Juno-AI, and ascending bars for Crowwd. Their vertices, colors, and supporting route line interpolate between states. Photos and plain project information sit beside the geometry. Direct links and a static choice list remain available.

**Current assessment:** a stronger geometric foundation than the deployed polygon cluster. The eventual implementation should develop richer project-specific illustration and tighten the typography choreography after a homepage direction is chosen.

## Recommendation for review

Start with **The Desk**. Its interaction uses Jay’s actual imagery and offers the clearest spatial bridge to a calm portfolio. Pair it with the project transformation study, then refine both together. Do not rebuild the remaining pages before Jay has reviewed the prototype behavior.
